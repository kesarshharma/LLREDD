SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION FOR TRAFFIC JAM ASSIST (TJA) SYSTEM

1. System Overview
The Traffic Jam Assist (TJA) system combines Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) and Lane Keeping Assist (LKA) to reduce driver workload during stop-and-go traffic scenarios up to 60 km/h.

2. Functional Requirements
REQ-TJA-001: The TJA system shall control longitudinal acceleration and deceleration based on front radar target distance.
REQ-TJA-002: The TJA system shall require active engagement of the driver hand-on-wheel sensor every 15 seconds.
REQ-TJA-003: The system shall issue a visual and acoustic driver takeover request if lane markings become indistinguishable for more than 2 seconds.
REQ-TJA-004: The TJA system shall bring the vehicle to a full stop when the preceding vehicle stops.
REQ-TJA-005: The system shall resume vehicle motion automatically if the preceding vehicle moves within 3 seconds of stopping.
REQ-TJA-006: If the preceding vehicle remains stopped for longer than 3 seconds, the driver must tap the accelerator pedal to resume TJA.
REQ-TJA-007: The TJA system shall disengage immediately upon driver brake pedal intervention.
REQ-TJA-008: The TJA system shall maintain a user-selected headway time gap of 1.0s, 1.5s, or 2.0s to the preceding vehicle.
REQ-TJA-009: The lateral control subsystem shall keep the vehicle centered within the detected lane boundaries.
REQ-TJA-010: The system shall verify sensor health status continuously before granting steering torque authorization.
