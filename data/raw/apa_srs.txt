SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION FOR AUTOMATED PARKING ASSIST (APA) SYSTEM

1. System Overview
The Automated Parking Assist (APA) system assists drivers in identifying suitable parallel and perpendicular parking spaces and controlling steering, throttle, and braking during parking maneuvers.

2. Functional Requirements
REQ-APA-001: The system shall always stop the vehicle to prevent collision with objects during the parking maneuver.
REQ-APA-002: The system shall start identifying available parking spots upon user confirmation.
REQ-APA-003: The system shall allow the customer to select an available parking spot displayed through the HMI with a confirm button.
REQ-APA-004: The APA system shall utilize 12 ultrasonic sensors and 4 surround view cameras to calculate target trajectory.
REQ-APA-005: The system shall abort the automatic parking procedure immediately if an unexpected obstacle enters the trajectory boundary within 0.5 meters.
REQ-APA-006: The APA system shall signal completion of the parking maneuver via audio chime and electronic park brake activation.
REQ-APA-007: The APA system shall limit maximum vehicle speed to 5 km/h during automated steering and maneuvering.
REQ-APA-008: The APA system shall support both parallel parking and perpendicular reverse parking modes.
REQ-APA-009: The system shall request steering control override permission from the primary vehicle domain controller prior to maneuver initiation.
REQ-APA-010: The system shall verify camera lens clarity and ultrasonic transducer health before enabling parking assist.
